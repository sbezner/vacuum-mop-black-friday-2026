# Vacuum Mop Black Friday 2026 Deals

A snapshot of wet-dry vacuum mop and robot vac-mop sales, checked Wednesday, Nov 25, 2026 (America/Chicago). Built so friends can open the list on a phone with no login.

**Live site:** https://sbezner.github.io/vacuum-mop-black-friday-2026/

The page lists 129 deals in a single scrolling results list, like a store search page. Each row has a product photo, the sale price, the regular price with the percent off, a star rating and review count when I could find a real one, and a button to the store product page. Search by model, brand, category, or store. Filter by category, brand, store, Editor's Choice, or a minimum 4-star rating on the left (on a phone, open Filters). Sort by featured, % savings, dollar savings, rating, or price. Prices change, so confirm the live price on the store page before you buy.

Ratings come from Amazon when I could match the same model, otherwise from the brand's official product page. If I could not find a rating for that exact model, the card says "No rating yet." Editor's Choice is a short list of machines rated 4.3 or higher, with at least 50 reviews, and a deep discount (generally 25% or more off). It is not a paid placement.
