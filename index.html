Buying tips

What these machines are

  • Cordless and corded wet-dry vac mops vacuum and wash hard floors in one pass, with separate clean and dirty water tanks. They are for sealed hard floors, not thick carpet.
  • Robot vac-mop combos vacuum and mop on their own. Many now wash and dry their own mops in the dock. They are a different kind of machine from a handheld floor washer.
  • A “was” price is the regular or list price shown on that store’s page. It is not always the typical street price from last month, so use it as a ceiling.

How I would choose

  • If you want one machine you push yourself, start with a cordless wet-dry such as a Tineco Floor One, Roborock F25, Dreame Aero, or Bissell CrossWave. The better ones lie flat under furniture and wash and dry their own roller.
  • If you want the floors done without you, look at the robot vac-mop section. Compare suction, mop washing, and whether the dock needs a drain or just tanks you empty.
  • Match the solution and roller to the exact model. Using the wrong formula, or a steam model’s roller in a non-steam machine, is a common way to wreck a unit.

Before you buy

  • Confirm the live price and stock on the product page. Black Friday prices can change, and I also dropped certified-refurbished robot listings from Roborock’s official catalog so this list stays on new machines. Friday, Nov 27, may still add doorbusters.
  • Amazon promo codes do not always apply on the first try. If a thread mentions a code, check that it actually came off at checkout.
  • Sam’s Club prices here are the member prices shown on their site.
